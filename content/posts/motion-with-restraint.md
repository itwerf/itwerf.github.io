---
title: Motion with restraint
date: 2025-11-12
summary: How to make interfaces feel alive without turning them into a carnival.
tags:
  - design
  - motion
---

Motion should clarify hierarchy, not decorate every pixel.

Start with three jobs: **enter**, **relate**, and **respond**. Enter tells the visitor where they are. Relate connects list items so the eye can scan. Respond confirms that a click or hover did something.

Skip particle storms. Prefer a soft hero shift, staggered cards, and transitions that get out of the way.
